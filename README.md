# Edge-disjoint clique packings

By Jialin He.

This repository contains four related, independent research manuscripts on edge-disjoint clique packings, covering graphs near the Turán threshold, sharp coefficients and densities under forbidden-clique conditions, and almost complete graphs.

1. [Second-order asymptotics for edge-disjoint clique packings](<Second-order asymptotics for edge-disjoint clique packings.pdf>) — 16 pages.

   For each fixed clique order, the paper determines the optimal second-order term for integer and fractional clique packings above the Turán threshold when the edge excess is superlinear and subquadratic in the number of vertices. It also characterizes the full range in which the minimum possible packing number is asymptotic to the edge excess.

2. [Sharp fractional clique packings in clique-free graphs](<Sharp fractional clique packings in clique-free graphs.pdf>) — 10 pages.

   The paper determines the best uniform linear coefficient in the fractional clique-packing bound for every pair of packing and forbidden-clique orders. It also determines the corresponding optimal asymptotic relative coefficient for integer packings with a fixed forbidden subgraph.

3. [Clique packing densities in graphs with forbidden cliques](<Clique packing densities in graphs with forbidden cliques.pdf>) — 10 pages.

   The paper determines the full asymptotic density function for K_r-packings in K_{r+1}-free graphs and for triangle packings in K_5-free graphs. It also gives a finite integer triangle-packing bound for K_4-free graphs and finite fractional refinements.

4. [Sharp clique packings in almost complete graphs](<Sharp clique packings in almost complete graphs.pdf>) — 19 pages.

   The paper determines sharp asymptotics for the edges left uncovered by optimal integer and fractional clique packings in almost complete graphs. The results include the contribution of degree divisibility, profiles for a linear number of missing edges, and stability when the number of missing edges is superlinear.

The two forbidden-clique manuscripts above replace the earlier manuscript titled *Clique packings in graphs with forbidden subgraphs*. Its linear coefficient results are incorporated into the general coefficient paper; the density paper retains the density bounds and finite refinements. The earlier PDF remains available in the repository history.
